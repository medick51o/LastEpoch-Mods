MedicK's Terrible Cooldowns v1.0.2
Season 5 compatibility update

- Fixed the CoreModule loading failure that could prevent the mod from starting after the Season 5 update.
- Built the compatibility repair into the mod DLL. No separate plugin, script, or extra library download is required.
- The repair preserves an original CoreModule backup and handles regenerated files when needed.
- Rebuilt against the current Season 5 game assemblies.
- Existing settings, custom labels, and panel position are preserved.

Installation: Close the game and replace medick_CooldownTracker.dll in Last Epoch/Mods. The ZIP contains exactly one DLL. Requires MelonLoader 0.7.3.

Press Home to open settings.
