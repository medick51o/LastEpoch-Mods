# Boss and Harbinger fact-check

Access date for every re-fetch in this pass: 2026-10-02.

This pass re-read the forum topic JSON, the Tunklab timelines index, the Temporal Sanctum dungeon page, the Heorot and Frost Lich NPC pages, the Herald of Oblivion page (`/npc/UberAterroth`), the Tunklab homepage footer, and the Tunklab client bundle `3nhe36ktaxpqv.js` (7,964,187 bytes, string `1.5-preview1` fourteen times, `1.3.6` once). `https://www.lastepoch.com/1-5-1/patchnotes` returned a Nuxt shell (`window.__NUXT__`) of 5,718 bytes with no note body. Last Epoch Tools and the EHG support articles cited by the lanes were not re-read (HTTP 403 or a Cloudflare challenge). `Il2CppLE.dll` is not in this workspace, so lane 4 signatures were not re-inspected.

Evidence classes: developer documentation (EHG forum posts), datamined-reference (Tunklab 1.5 preview extract), community (Maxroll and player posts), unknown (pages not re-read). A preview extract is not a 1.5.1 client dump. A 2023 or 2024 note is not current behavior by itself.

The damage-array order used by lanes 2 and 3 (Physical, Fire, Cold, Lightning, Necrotic, Void, Poison) was not re-proven from an EHG page or from the DLL. Index positions below are literal. Element names for those indexes stay a convention.

- Lagon: 0.9 names stay historical. Current preview skills are the five shared ids. Tips that mention telegraphs and Tidal Wave movement were deleted. Damage types stay empty.
- Emperor of Corpses: distance-from-center stays. Element, detonation timing, and the 1.4.2 cut do not. Tips deleted. Damage types stay empty.

- Harbinger of Hatred: the 1.2.1 sentence stays a visual-versus-damage fix. The telegraph tip was deleted. One sentence remains: a Harbinger imitation is not the original boss's full move set. Damage types stay empty.

New profiles were not added to `BossCatalog.cs`. They are in the JSON catalog only.
