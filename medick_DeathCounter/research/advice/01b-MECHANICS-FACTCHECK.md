# Fact-check of `01-MECHANICS.md`

Checked on 2026-10-02 against the Eleventh Hour Games support articles (the public copy of the in-game guide), the formula images on those articles, forum.lastepoch.com dev posts and patch notes, Maxroll's Defenses Explained (last updated 2026-04-02), and the URLs the mechanics note itself cites. The live lastepochtools guide HTML is behind a Cloudflare challenge, so the guide mirror was checked through the image files that site publishes and through the copy embedded in EHG's own articles. Game code was not changed.

Current season label: Season 5, Rage of the Frostborn, patch 1.5, live 2026-10-01. Hotfix 1.5.0.1 went out the same day. Patch 1.1.3 is the 2024-07-25 patch. It is not Season 5.

The support articles that carry the armor, ward, dodge, block, and freeze formulas were last updated 2026-07-13 or 2026-07-14, before the Season 5 launch. The Season 5 patch-note page (https://lastepoch.com/1-5/patchnotes) is a client-rendered page and did not return the note body to a plain fetch. Nothing in the Season 5 forum thread discusses these formulas. Treat a Season 5 formula change as still unchecked (see the last section).

## Corrected summary

Use this table in the advice engine. `a` is area level. For armor, dodge, and block, `x` is that stat's rating.

| Topic | Rule to use |
| --- | --- |
| Resistance cap | 75%. Values above 75% do not reduce damage by themselves. |
| Area penetration | Enemies only. 1% per area level, maximum 75% at area level 75 and above. Applied after the 75% cap, so it can push resistance negative. Overcap does not counter it. Players do not gain this penetration. |
| Shred, shock, Marked for Death | These lower the resistance stat before the cap, so overcap does absorb them. Resistance shred: 5% per stack, 2% against players and bosses, 10 stacks, 4 seconds. Shock: 2% lightning resistance per stack on players and bosses, 10 stacks, 4 seconds, and it also raises stun chance. Marked for Death: -25% all resistances, 8 seconds, 1 stack. |
| Armor | Hits only. Use the guide formula in the verdict table. It approaches 85% for physical and does not exceed it. Non-physical mitigation is 70% of the physical result, so its ceiling is 59.5%. No effect on damage over time. |
| Ward decay | Use the quadratic formula in the verdict table, on ward above the decay threshold. 100% ward retention counts as 1 in the denominator. Intelligence is 2% ward retention per point. |
| Ward and health | Ordinary damage, including ordinary damage over time, is removed from ward before health. Endurance never reduces the ward portion. |
| Endurance | Starts at 20% less damage. Cap 60% less damage, multiplicative with other modifiers. Threshold starts at 20% of max health and has no cap. Only health below the threshold is reduced, including the part of a larger hit that crosses the line. Hits and damage over time both qualify. |
| Crits | Enemy crit multiplier 200%. Avoidance is rolled after the attacker's crit chance. 100% less bonus damage taken from crits is a hard cap and makes the crit deal a normal hit. Damage over time cannot crit. |
| Dodge | Use the guide formula in the verdict table. It approaches 85% from rating, and the guide says it cannot go above 85%. Not applied to damage over time. A dodge is not a hit. |
| Block | Effectiveness uses the guide formula in the verdict table and cannot remove more than 85% of the hit. Block chance is separate. Spells can be blocked. Damage over time cannot. A block still counts as a hit. |
| Glancing blow | 35% less damage on that hit. Chance above 100% does nothing. Not applied to damage over time. A glancing blow still counts as a hit. Dusk Shroud grants chance. Silver Shroud does not. |
| Damaging ailments | Ignite, bleed, poison, damned, and electrify have no stack cap. Doom caps at 4. Time Rot caps at 12. Poison shreds poison resistance on the first 30 stacks only, at 2% per stack against players and bosses. |
| Freeze | `(freeze rate x freeze rate multiplier) / (max health + current ward)`, then the chill, frostbite, and boss adjustments in the verdict row. The page's own example turns a 500% multiplier into a factor of 6. |
| Stun | A non-player hit needs more than 5% of max health to be able to stun when increased stun chance and stun avoidance are both 0. Each 100 stun avoidance raises that threshold by 1% of max health. Players start at 250 + 5 per level. Do not add ward, and do not add avoidance as flat health, unless an in-game test overturns the support article. |
| Bosses | Boss Ward notches, not the old 2% reduction per 1% health lost. A consumed notch grants ward from max health and a short stun. That ward's decay speeds up over time. |
| Corruption | Empowered timelines start at 100 and have no maximum. They raise monster health, monster damage, experience, and item rarity. Standard timelines cap at 50. Do not hard-code the 1.0 percentages until they are rechecked after Season 2's damage-over-time change. |
| Layer math | Resistance, armor, block effectiveness, and glancing blow multiply. Endurance applies only to the health portion below the threshold. There is no official dodge-then-block order. |
| Death screen | Since patch 1.1.3 (2024-07-25) the screen shows killing-blow damage, overkill past 0 health, and whether that blow crit. The property names on `ReceiveDetailedDeathInfo` are not in the patch notes. |
| Version | Season 5 is patch 1.5 (hotfix 1.5.0.1), live 2026-10-01. Patch 1.1.3 is July 2024. |

Worked armor check, area level 100, 1000 armor. Guide formula: about 23.7% physical, about 16.6% for a non-physical hit. The note's formula: about 90.8%, then clamped to 85%.

Worked dodge check, area level 100, 1000 dodge rating. Guide formula: about 29.1%. The note's formula: about 54.5%.

## Still unverifiable, and how to test it in game

1. Season 5 formula edits. The July 2026 support images predate the 2026-10-01 launch, and https://lastepoch.com/1-5/patchnotes did not return a readable body. Open that page in a browser and search for armor, dodge, block, ward, endurance, freeze, stun, and corruption. If none of those sections change a formula, the July images still stand.

2. Area level versus monster level. The guide uses area level. Maxroll's penetration paragraph says enemy level. In an echo, read the area level on the map, find a monster whose level differs, and compare the character sheet's armor and dodge percents with the formulas using each level. Sheet values follow whichever input the game actually uses.

3. Armor coefficient 0.0015 versus the older 0.0012 tools image. On the character sheet at a known area level and armor total, compute both versions. The July 2026 support image is 0.0015. Prefer the value that matches the sheet.

4. Ward retention units. Gain a round amount of ward retention (for example 100% from the sheet). With threshold 0, time how fast a large ward pool falls. If 100% retention makes the denominator 1.5, the sheet percent is divided by 100 before it enters the formula. If the pool barely moves, the formula is being fed the raw percent and this note's unit reading is wrong.

5. Ward and percent-of-current-health ground damage. On a ward build, stand in a pool that the tooltip describes as a percent of current health, such as the Emperor of Corpses blood. If health falls while ward does not, Maxroll's bypass is still in the game.

6. Freeze multiplier. Take a skill whose tooltip shows a known freeze rate and a known freeze rate multiplier. Freeze a normal enemy with known health and no ward, and a boss. Check whether 500% on the tooltip behaves as x6, as in the guide example, or as x5. Include a target that has ward and confirm the denominator grew by that ward.

7. Stun threshold. At level 1 the built-in avoidance is 255, which is already more than 0, so the clean "0 avoidance" case is hard to reach. Compare two hits of known size against the same health total, one with extra avoidance and one without, and repeat with a large ward pool up. If ward does not change the stun, the current support article is right and the old wiki formula is not. Each 100 extra avoidance should demand about 1% more of max health.

8. Death-report fields. The 1.1.3 notes confirm the screen, not the IL2CPP names. Log every argument of `PlayerActorSync.ReceiveDetailedDeathInfo` on a death. Check whether the crit flag is `wasCrit` or one of the other names `ArgReader` already probes. Compare the damage number with health and ward actually lost, to see post-mitigation damage versus a pre-mitigation roll. Repeat a ward death and a low-damage killing blow after a damage-over-time effect, which is the test the mechanics note already describes.

9. Corruption curve after Season 2. The support page confirms the direction (more health, more damage, more experience, more rarity) and the 100 / no-maximum empowered bounds. It does not reprint the 1.0 percentages, and Season 2 cut the damage-over-time half of that bonus. Read the corruption tooltip in an empowered echo at 100 and at 200. If it still says 60% more health and damage at 100, record whether damage over time is half of that, as the Season 2 post described.

10. Boss Ward numbers. The guide says a notch grants ward "based on their maximum health" and does not give the fraction or the decay rate. On a timeline boss, record health when a notch breaks, the ward gained, and how fast that ward falls. That replaces the retired 2% reduction rule. Do not tune advice around the old adaptive mitigation.
